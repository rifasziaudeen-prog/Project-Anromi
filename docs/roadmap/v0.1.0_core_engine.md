# Phase Map: v0.1.0 — Core Engine & 16-Realm Mathematical Foundation

**Target Version:** `0.1.0`  
**Milestone Status:** ✅ **Completed**  
**Milestone Goal:** Strip the prototype game framework and build the comprehensive mathematical engine for the 16 Canonical Cultivation Realms, sub-stage progression, meridian capacities, bottleneck calculations, and session verification.

---

## 🎯 Scope & Objectives
- [x] **16-Realm Enum & Data Registry:** Define all 16 realms with their sub-stage schemas:
  - **Realms 1 to 8 (Mortal & Earth Steps):** 9 Layers + Great Circle (`[Layer 1 ... Layer 9, Great Circle]`). Bottlenecks at Layer 3->4, Layer 6->7, Layer 9->Great Circle, and Great Circle->Next Realm.
  - **Realms 9 to 15 (Heaven & Immortal Steps):** 4 Sub-stages: `[Early Stage, Middle Stage, Late Stage, Peak Stage]`.
  - **Realm 16 (Eternal Transcendent):** Absolute Supreme Perfection.
- [x] **Cultivator Core State Model:**
  - Lifespan counter ($L_{curr} / L_{max}$) scaled by realm.
  - Meridian Capacity ($Q_{max}$) and Stored Qi ($Q_{curr}$).
  - Qi Purity Ratio ($P_{qi} \in [0.1, 1.0]$).
  - Bottleneck Status (`is_bottleneck: bool`, `bottleneck_comprehension: float`).
  - Meridian damage debuff counter ($0.0$ to $1.0$).
  - Dao Heart stability and Demonic Sin counter.
- [x] **Meditation & Absorption Math Engine:**
  - Ambient Leyline Qi density absorption with logarithmic diminishing returns:
    $$\Delta Q = \text{BaseRate} \times \text{RootMultiplier} \times \text{LeylineDensity} \times (1 - \frac{Q_{curr}}{Q_{max}})^{0.35} \times \text{MeridianEff}$$
- [x] **Breakthrough Probability Engine:**
  - Formula factoring Qi Purity, Meridian Stability, Dao Heart, and Bottleneck insight:
    $$P_{success} = \text{BaseChance}(Realm, Stage) + \text{FullnessBonus} + \text{PurityBonus} + \text{DaoHeartMod} - \text{MeridianPenalty} + \text{InsightBonus} + \text{PillModifier}$$
- [x] **Failure Backlash Calculations:**
  - Minor Failure: Loss of $25\%$ accumulated Qi and minor meridian stress (+0.05).
  - Moderate Failure: Loss of $50\%$ Qi + Meridian Rupture (+0.25) + Dao Heart damage (-10).
  - Catastrophic Failure: Loss of $80\%$ Qi + severe rupture (+0.50) + Dao Heart trauma (-25) + potential Layer/Realm regression.
- [x] **FastAPI REST Endpoints Refactor:**
  - `POST /api/cultivator/register` — Initial root rolling and meridian creation.
  - `GET /api/cultivator/profile/{discord_id}` — Full 16-realm status, lifespan, Qi stats, and bottleneck state.
  - `POST /api/cultivator/meditate/{discord_id}` — Perform simulated meditation cycle with ambient density multiplier.
  - `GET /api/cultivator/breakthrough-odds/{discord_id}` — Transparent probability breakdown.
  - `POST /api/cultivator/breakthrough/{discord_id}` — Attempt sub-stage or realm breakthrough.
